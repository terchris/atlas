Some\norganisations — including local branches of unitary NGOs such as Frelsesarmeen\nand Kirkens Bymisjon — are registered as underenheter rather than as enheter,\nso a copy of enheter alone has a population gap exactly where Atlas's NGO work\ncares (docs/research/ngo-research/ingestion-specs/brreg-underenheter.md).\n\nThis is the **bootstrap** load: a complete point-in-time copy.
