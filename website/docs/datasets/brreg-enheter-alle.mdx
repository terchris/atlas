## About this source

<div style={{whiteSpace: 'pre-wrap'}}>{"Brønnøysundregistrene's Enhetsregisteret holds every legal entity registered in\nNorway — companies, foundations, associations, public bodies and sole\nproprietorships — around 1.17 million of them. Brreg regenerates a complete\ngzipped JSON export daily; Atlas downloads that file, streams it, and stores each\nrecord verbatim as jsonb in `raw.brreg_enheter_snapshot`, keyed on\norganisasjonsnummer.\n\nAtlas previously held 122 organisational units, selected by a curated list of 11\nNGOs. Holding the whole register instead means any Norwegian organisation can be\nresolved from its organisasjonsnummer without a new ingest, and it is what makes\norganisational data from unrelated sources join at all.\n\nThis is the **bootstrap** load: a complete point-in-time copy. Keeping it current\nis the change feed's job (PLAN-002, `/oppdateringer/enheter`), which reads only\nwhat moved. Underenheter (~863k sub-units) are a separate follow-on.\n\nNothing is flattened, renamed or re-encoded on the way in. Typed columns for the\nfields Atlas actually queries are built in dbt (PLAN-003).\n"}</div>
