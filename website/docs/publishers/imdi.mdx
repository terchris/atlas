# Integrerings- og mangfoldsdirektoratet

<img src="/img/publishers/imdi.png" alt="Integrerings- og mangfoldsdirektoratet logo" style={{maxWidth: '200px', marginBottom: '1rem'}} />

The Directorate of Integration and Diversity. Publishes refugee
resettlement figures (bosettingstall) and immigration statistics by
reason and country background, per kommune and year. Atlas's existing
FHI-mediated immigration figures (innvkat, innvandrere) describe *who*;
IMDi's data is the *when/where* resettlement-flow signal neither of
those carries.


[Visit Integrerings- og mangfoldsdirektoratet →](https://www.imdi.no/)
