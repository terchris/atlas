VAT-compensation allocations to voluntary organisations
(momskompensasjon) — the financial-resolution half of the Norwegian
NGO sector that Brreg's own Frivillighetsregisteret (status/ICNPO
category, already in `dim_brreg_enhet`) does not carry on its own.
