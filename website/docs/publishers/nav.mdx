# Arbeids- og velferdsdirektoratet

<img src="/img/publishers/nav.svg" alt="Arbeids- og velferdsdirektoratet logo" style={{maxWidth: '200px', marginBottom: '1rem'}} />

The Norwegian Labour and Welfare Administration. Publishes open
labour-market and benefits statistics — registered unemployment,
disability benefit (uføretrygd), work-assessment allowance (AAP) and
sick-leave figures — mostly as monthly Excel releases per kommune.
`display_name` is the formal directorate name; the public brand is
"NAV", used in `publisher:` on every NAV manifest to match.


[Visit Arbeids- og velferdsdirektoratet →](https://www.nav.no/)
