# Statistisk sentralbyrå

<img src="/img/publishers/ssb.svg" alt="Statistisk sentralbyrå logo" style={{maxWidth: '200px', marginBottom: '1rem'}} />

Norway's national statistics office. Maintains the StatBank
(statistikkbanken) — the canonical source of demographic, economic,
and social indicators at kommune, fylke, and national resolution.
Data released under NLOD; tables identified by numeric IDs preserved
with leading zeros (e.g. table 07459).


[Visit Statistisk sentralbyrå →](https://www.ssb.no/)
