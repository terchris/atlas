# Utdanningsdirektoratet

<img src="/img/publishers/udir.png" alt="Utdanningsdirektoratet logo" style={{maxWidth: '200px', marginBottom: '1rem'}} />

The Directorate for Education and Training. Publishes school-level
register data (GSI — Grunnskolens Informasjonssystem), the annual
pupil survey (Elevundersøkelsen: trivsel, mobbing) and absence
statistics, at a sharper resolution than Atlas's existing FHI-mediated
bullying figure (`fhi-mobbing`, a 3-year rolling 7th+10th-grade
aggregate).


[Visit Utdanningsdirektoratet →](https://www.udir.no/)
