# Posten Bring

<img src="/img/publishers/bring.svg" alt="Posten Bring logo" style={{maxWidth: '200px', marginBottom: '1rem'}} />

Norway's national postal and logistics operator. Publishes the
canonical Norwegian postcode register (Postnummerregister), with a
category per postcode (street address, post box, service address)
that SSB's own kommune/fylke classifications do not carry.
