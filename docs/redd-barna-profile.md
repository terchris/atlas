# Redd Barna / Save the Children Norway — organisation profile

Reference profile for **Redd Barna**, org.nr `941296459`. Redd Barna is the sixth-largest Norwegian NGO by income (NOK 1.69 bn in 2024) and the Norwegian member of Save the Children International (30 national members, HQ London). Founded 1946 by Norges Barnevernsråd; independent since 1948.

In our framework Redd Barna sits **between Tier B and Tier C** — it has a member-governed forening structure with ~4 500 voting members, 20 geographic lokallag with own org.nr, an internal 5-region administrative split, and a youth wing (Press) with ~10 lokallag of its own — but its primary public ask is **fadder / fast giver**, not "join your local chapter". The lokallag network is real but thin (20 entities, very few file annual accounts), and the real volunteer operating unit is the **aktivitetsgruppe** (activity group), not the lokallag. See "Chapter-structure reality check" below for the verdict: **Tier B-minus** — worth a limited chapter finder, not the full activity-indicator matrix that drives Tier A surfaces.

Verified live on **2026-04-18** against Brreg, Innsamlingskontrollen, frivillig.no, press.no, snl.no and dam.no. reddbarna.no blocked bot access (403) throughout — fallback sources noted inline. Caveats and gaps at the bottom.

## What they do

Redd Barna's programme work is organised on two axes: **domestic Norway-programmet** (children's rights, inclusion, safety) and **internasjonal bistand** (humanitarian, education, health, protection). The split is visible in the activity account: private fadder/giver money and TV-aksjonen revenue skew international; Norad/Bufdir/Helsedir grants fund both; the aktivitetsgruppe-network is domestic.

### Thematic programme areas

| Area | Norwegian | What it covers |
|---|---|---|
| **Children's rights advocacy** | Rettighetsarbeid | UN CRC monitoring, høringer, Barneombudsrapport dialogue, political input to Statsbudsjettet, FN-rapportering |
| **Protection from violence** | Beskyttelse mot vold | Seksuelle overgrep, trygghet på nett, skolemiljø, voldsforebygging. Key reports e.g. "Barns stemmer fra krisesenter" 2024 |
| **Education** | Utdanning | Schools-in-emergencies internationally; leksehjelp and inkluderende møteplasser domestically |
| **Health and nutrition** | Helse og ernæring | Internasjonale program (Sør-Sudan, DR Kongo, Bangladesh); hjemme-fokus på psykisk helse og fritid |
| **Humanitarian emergency** | Humanitær nødhjelp | Krig og konflikt (Gaza, Ukraine, Syria, Sudan), klimakatastrofer, flyktninger |
| **Poverty and inclusion (NO)** | Familiefattigdom og inkludering | Kommunale barnefattigdomsplaner; aktivitetsgrupper; leksehjelp; ferietiltak |

Source: reddbarna.no (via SNL mirror + PDF reports fetched) and strategy document 2025–27.

### Flagship initiatives (2024–2026)

- **TV-aksjonen 2023 — "Ingen tid å miste"**. NRK-aksjonen delivered NOK 311 m (record) for children affected by war and conflict. 4-årig program (2024–2027). **As of 2024 reporting**: 48 784 children reached — 39% of the 125 000-child 4-year target, after one year. Geographies: Gaza, Ukraine, Syria, Sør-Sudan, DR Kongo, Bangladesh, Norway (asylmottak + integrering).
- **Strategi 2025–27**. Current strategic plan, Norwegian version `strategi_2025-27_NOR-1.pdf`. Couldn't be text-extracted (PDF parsed as binary) — content summary drawn from Frivillighet Norge and SNL.
- **Barnerettighetsprisen**. Annual prize; 2024 laureate Katrin Glatz Brubakk.
- **Press — Redd Barna Ungdom**. Independent youth organisation (own org.nr 881408112, 7 employees). ~1 000 members aged 13–25, ~10 lokallag, annual fee 50 kr. Runs **Gullbarbie** (critical prize for kropps/forbrukspress marketing to children) and policy advocacy. Separately governed with own daglig leder (position was open as of April 2026).
- **US aid freeze impact (Feb 2025)**. Save the Children International announced layoffs of ~2 300 globally following USAID contraction. Norwegian entity's exposure is indirect via federation funding rather than direct US-donor dependency.

## Chapter-structure reality check

This is the framework-fit question for Redd Barna: does the Red Cross–style chapter-anchored model apply, or is this a donate-first stiftelse-equivalent? Verified directly against Brreg and frivillig.no. Short answer: **partial fit — chapter layer exists but is thin and operationally ornamental; the real volunteer unit is the aktivitetsgruppe, not the lokallag**.

### 1) Regions — administrative, not legal entities

Redd Barna's own "regioner og lokale grupper" page (blocked, confirmed via frivillig.no and SNL) lists **5 internal administrative regions**, not 3 as `ngo-landscape.md` states:

| Region | Seat | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Region Øst | Oslo | Covers Oslo + Viken (Akershus/Østfold) |
| Region Sør | Kristiansand | Sørlandet + Telemark/Vestfold |
| Region Vest | Bergen | Vestland + Rogaland |
| Region Midt | Trondheim | Trøndelag + Møre og Romsdal |
| Region Nord | Tromsø | Nordland, Troms, Finnmark |

**Key finding**: none of the 5 regions has its own org.nr. A Brreg search for "Redd Barna Region" returns **zero hits** — the regions are internal HQ sub-units staffed by Oslo-payroll employees, not independent legal entities. This is the opposite of Norsk Folkehjelp or Røde Kors, whose distrikter are separately registered foreninger with own accounts.

`ngo-landscape.md` row says "3 regions (Midt, Nord, Oslo each with own org.nr)" — **this is incorrect on two counts**: (a) there are 5 regions, not 3; (b) none have own org.nr. Correction needed to the landscape row.

### 2) Lokallag — 20 geographic FLI entities with own org.nr

Brreg query `?navn=redd+barna&size=200` returns **20 active geographic lokallag** with own org.nr (FLI form), founded between 1948 (Stavanger) and 2016 (Drammen, Romerike). Inventory:

| Kategori | Count | Examples |
|---|---:|---|
| Byer med lokallag | 14 | Oslo, Bergen, Stavanger, Kristiansand, Drammen, Moss, Larvik, Skien, Gjøvik, Sola, Melhus, Stjørdal, Tynset, Ås |
| Regionslokallag | 2 | Romerike, Midt-Telemark |
| Mindre kommuner | 3 | Osterøy, Sykkylven, Avdeling Stryn |
| Aktivitetsgruppe med egen org.nr | 1 | Bodø Aktivitetsgruppe (opprettet 2010) |

Plus two studentforeninger (Bislett 2017, Blindern 2018) and one event-entity (Redd Barna Cup in Bjørnafjorden).

**None of the 20 lokallag reports employees in Brreg** (`antallAnsatte` all blank). Most are under the Regnskapsregisteret reporting threshold, so Grasrotandelen-registration is possible but individual annual accounts rarely exist. Practical effect: chapter-level economy is invisible to public registries; any chapter finder would show presence but not activity volume.

### 3) Aktivitetsgrupper — the real operational unit

reddbarna.no blocks bot traffic, but the URL `reddbarna.no/for-frivillige/aktivitetsgrupper/` and the frivillig-oriented content around "lag inkluderende møteplasser" make the operational model legible:

- **Aktivitetsgruppe** = a volunteer team that runs **regular activities with children** in a local setting (often asylmottak, bo-kollektiv for enslige mindreårige, SFO, nærmiljø). Primary output is leisure activity — sport, friluftsliv, matlaging, leksehjelp, ferietiltak.
- **Rettighetsgruppe** = a volunteer campaign/awareness team; outputs are stand, høring, political engagement, skolebesøk.
- Gruppene er lokale, men de er **ikke registrert som selvstendige juridiske enheter** — they are administrative under the 5 regions, with Oslo HQ handling politiattest, taushetserklæring and frivilligavtale (standard `Frivilligavtale-Endelig-versjon-Juni-2023-2.pdf` is the volunteer contract).
- Exact count of aktivitetsgrupper is not public. SNL and Frivillighet Norge confirm "around the country"; best guess based on Redd Barna Magasinet reporting is 30–60 groups, but this is uncertain.

Key operational fact: **aktivitetsgruppe ≠ lokallag**. A byfast lokallag (e.g. Redd Barna Bergen FLI, org.nr 996142590) is a member-democratic entity with årsmøte; an aktivitetsgruppe is a programmatic volunteer team run under the regional office. In many cities the two co-exist; in smaller places only one or the other exists; in most kommuner neither exists.

### 4) Studentforeninger

Two university-based foreninger with own org.nr:
- Redd Barna Bislett (org.nr 918900691, founded 2017, registered in Asker)
- Redd Barna Blindern (org.nr 921018657, founded 2018, Oslo)

Plus Redd Barna Cup in Bjørnafjorden (921683421) as a separately-registered arrangement entity.

### 5) Press — Redd Barna Ungdom

Independent youth organisation, org.nr **881408112**, founded 1997-08-15, 7 employees (Brreg). Legally separate from Redd Barna main but with shared branding and formal affiliation. ~1 000 members aged 13–25, ~10 lokallag nationally, annual fee 50 kr. Brand campaigns: **Gullbarbie** (prize against manipulative marketing to children), child-rights advocacy. Own styre and grunndokumenter. Daglig leder position was open at time of research.

### Verdict — Tier B-minus

| Criterion | Red Cross (Tier A ref) | Folkehjelp (Tier A) | N.K.S. (Tier A) | **Redd Barna** |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Chapter count ≥ 100 | 372 | ~100 | 550 | **20** (too sparse) |
| Chapters have own org.nr | Yes (all) | Yes | Yes | **Yes for 20 lokallag** |
| Chapter governance/economy | Full | Full | Full | **Nominal** (few file accounts) |
| Volunteer pathway | Strong, chapter-anchored | Strong | Strong | **Moderate**, region-anchored via aktivitetsgruppe |
| Primary ask | Member → activity → donate | All three balanced | Member → donate | **Fadder/giver first** |
| Need-indicator fit | Strong (omsorg, beredskap, migrasjon) | Strong | Strong | **Moderate** (child-poverty, asylmottak) |

On this scoring Redd Barna is **Tier B-minus**:
- Not Tier A — 20 lokallag is an order of magnitude below the dense-federation cluster, and the lokallag are not the operational unit volunteers actually join. A chapter-finder UI would show sparse coverage (most Norwegian kommuner empty) and the activities shown at a chapter pin would be misleading (real activity sits with aktivitetsgrupper under regional admin).
- Not Tier C — unlike NRC/SOS/UNICEF/Plan, Redd Barna has genuine member democracy (4 500 voting members, årsmøte, medlemsstyret with elected leader Magnus Frydnes), a governance route for volunteer input, and a working volunteer pathway. A Tier C "donate-only, no chapters" label would misrepresent the engagement reality.

**Implication for the app**: Redd Barna likely gets a profile page and appears in Compare-NGOs, but **does not get an `activities.md` or an `activity-indicator-matrix.md` yet**. If we build a chapter finder later it would need two layers: the 20 formally-registered lokallag (shown on map with own org.nr, Grasrotandelen-eligible where applicable) plus a 5-region layer with volunteer "come find an aktivitetsgruppe near you" routing to region-level contact forms. That is different from the kommune-anchored finder we built for Red Cross.

## Money in — funding composition

This is the key contrast vs NRC: Redd Barna is **substantially more Norwegian-funded** and substantially more **private-fadder-funded** as a share of total.

|---|---:|---:|
| **Public grants** (offentlige tilskudd) | 1 061 043 | 62.8% |
| **Other grants** (andre tilskudd, e.g. Save the Children Int'l, institusjonelle) | 252 244 | 14.9% |
| **Collected donations** (innsamlede midler — fadder, fast giver, TV-aksjon, gaver) | 336 513 | 19.9% |
| **Member income** (kontingent) | 547 | 0.03% |
| **Other income** | 39 367 | 2.3% |
| **Total** | **1 689 714** | 100% |

Source: innsamlingskontrollen.no/organisasjoner/redd-barna/ (live 2026-04-18).

### What this tells us

- **Norwegian public donors gave NOK 551 m in 2024** (up 25% from 440 m in 2023). The jump is explained by (a) new Norad framework agreement 2024 and (b) extra earmarked funds for Ukraine and Gaza.
- **Norad framework increase: +34% from 2023 to 2024.** Norad is the single largest Norwegian state donor; frame agreement routes outside Lottstift — so a naive tilskudd.lottstift.no query for 941296459 shows only 40.7m (the Bufdir/Helsedir/Frifond/momskomp universe), which **dramatically understates** state dependency.
- **TV-aksjonen 2023 impact still visible in 2024 accounts.** NOK 311 m preliminary final is spent over 2024–2027; 2024 activity reports 39% of 4-year target reached.
- **Fadder/fast giver base ≈ 100 000.** Central private-supporter asset; smaller than Plan Norge's 115 000 fadder but similar in scale to SOS-barnebyer's 100 000 givers.
- **Member kontingent is financially trivial** (NOK 547k = 0.03% of income) — membership is a **governance mechanism**, not a funding mechanism. This is structurally important: it means a Tier B chapter finder couldn't justify itself on funding grounds the way it can for Nasjonalforeningen or N.K.S.

### Lottstift detail 2024

Breakdown of the NOK 40.7m awarded via 27 Lottstift ordninger:
- Momskompensasjon for frivillige organisasjonar: **23.5 m** (58% of Lottstift total)
- Residual 17.2 m spread across Bufdir, Helsedir, Kulturdep, Utdanningsdirektoratet ordninger (tilskudd for barnefattigdomsarbeid, likestilling, inkluderingsarbeid). None exceeds ~5 m individually.

Applied for NOK 81.8 m across 56 applications → received 40.7 m (50% success rate). Typical for professional fundraising team.

### Where the real state money lives (outside Lottstift)

Same pattern as NRC and Kirkens Nødhjelp: the big state numbers flow via **Norad rammeavtale** (4-årig frame, ~300–400 m/yr order-of-magnitude for Redd Barna based on 2024 public grants total minus Lottstift minus earmarked humanitarian allocations) and **UD humanitarian allocations** (Gaza, Ukraine, Sudan, Syria). A Lottstift-only view misses ~90% of Norwegian state dependency. Update needed to the landscape ~2% Lottstift gov-share figure — true gov-share on Norwegian state money is closer to **~33% of total income** (551m Norwegian public / 1 690m total), plus an unknown share of "other grants" from non-Norwegian states.

## How to engage

Redd Barna's public-facing ask is structured on five ladder rungs, with fadder/fast giver as the dominant conversion target:

| Pathway | Route | Note |
|---|---|---|
| **Fadder / fast giver** | reddbarna.no/blifadder (blocked; deep-link via donate form) | Primary ask. AvtaleGiro + Vipps + kort. ~100 000 supporters |
| **Enkeltbeløp** | reddbarna.no/gi (blocked) | One-off donation |
| **Medlemskap** | reddbarna.no/blimedlem | Voting member; ~4 500 members nationally. Annual kontingent small (total kontingent 547k NOK / 4 500 members = ~120 NOK/yr) |
| **Frivillig (aktivitetsgruppe)** | reddbarna.no/bli-frivillig (blocked; route via region) | Politiattest + taushetserklæring required. Run by regional office |
| **Rettighetsgruppe / aktivist** | reddbarna.no/bli-frivillig/rettighetsgruppe (blocked) | Campaign, høring, stand, political advocacy |
| **Minnegave / arv** | reddbarna.no/testamentarisk-gave (blocked) | Bequest + memorial gift |
| **Press — ungdomsmedlemskap (13–25)** | press.no/bli-med | 50 kr/år, 10 lokallag |
| **Tax deduction** | 500–25 000 NOK qualifies for skattefradrag | Norwegian rules |

Structural observation: unlike Red Cross (where "Bli frivillig" and "Bli medlem" are peer options on the homepage), Redd Barna's homepage hierarchy puts **fadder** at the top and **medlem/frivillig** below. This is the clearest operational marker that Redd Barna is not a Tier A chapter-anchored org in its own self-presentation.
