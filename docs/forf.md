# FORF — Frivillige organisasjoners redningsfaglige forum

Umbrella / interest forum for the volunteer organisations in the Norwegian rescue service (redningstjenesten). FORF is not itself an operational rescue organisation — it coordinates input to authorities, develops shared professional competence, and represents the volunteer side of the public rescue model.

Tagline: *"Lokal kompetanse — lokal trygghet."*

## 2. Purpose

FORF works as an **interest forum** for volunteer organisations in the rescue service. Stated functions:

- Collect and channel member perspectives into national authorities (Justis- og beredskapsdepartementet, Hovedredningssentralen, politiet)
- Advance shared professional competence (redningsfaglig utvikling) across member organisations
- Foster collaboration with the rescue authorities (HRS, LRS) and operational partnerships across member orgs
- Increase public visibility of volunteer contributions in the rescue service
- Develop the framework conditions (tilskuddsordninger, refusjonsregler, juridisk rammeverk)

Aggregate footprint represented: **500+ operational units** organising **10 000+ personnel** across the nine member organisations.

## 5. Position in Norwegian rescue model

Norway's rescue service (*den norske redningstjenesten*) is a **public, integrated, coordinated** model:

- **Coordination**: Hovedredningssentralen (HRS Sør-Norge, Bodø) for sea/luft and major land aksjoner; lokale redningssentraler (LRS) under politimesteren for typical land cases.
- **Resources**: state (politi, forsvar, helse), commercial (e.g. operatører av Sea King-erstatningen), and **voluntary** (FORF members).
- The voluntary side performs the **bulk of operational redningstjeneste-personell-timer** in Norway. Politiet/HRS lead; volunteers execute most of the ground.

FORF is the formal channel between this volunteer base and the state. State grants to volunteer rescue orgs are governed by *Forskrift om tilskudd til frivillige organisasjoner i redningstjenesten* (FOR-2020-12-10-2679), administered via Justis- og beredskapsdepartementet.

## 6. Why FORF matters for an NGO-explorer / Red Cross-anchored framework

- **Shared chapter substrate**: FORF members have overlapping local geography (a kommune may host a Røde Kors Hjelpekorps unit, a Norsk Folkehjelp Sanitet lokallag, an NRH-distrikt, a Redningsselskapet station). A multi-org chapter finder that respects rescue-cluster co-location is useful.
- **Common funding stream**: the FOR-2020-12-10-2679 forskrift creates a single Lottstift / JD-administered tilskuddsordning serving all member orgs. Already exposes per-org grant data via `tilskudd.lottstift.no`.
- **Joint activity taxonomy**: search-and-rescue, sanitet, samband, grotteredning, alpinredning are common across members. The activity-indicator matrix in [redcross-activity-indicator-matrix.md](redcross-activity-indicator-matrix.md) under "Beredskap" generalises across most FORF members with minor relabelling.
- **Crisis-band relevance**: FORF members are Norway's primary volunteer crisis-response capacity. Any crisis-band UI that surfaces "what local volunteer rescue capacity exists near me" naturally aggregates across FORF, not just one member.
