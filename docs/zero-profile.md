# ZERO — organisation profile

Reference profile for **Miljøstiftelsen ZERO / Zero Emission Resource Organisation**, org.nr `984143028`. ZERO is a small Oslo-based climate-policy foundation (~25 employees, ~NOK 30 m income) whose outsized public footprint comes from one flagship — **Zerokonferansen**, Norway's largest annual climate-policy conference — and a persistent presence in Norwegian transport, energy and industry policy debate.

In our framework ZERO is a **Tier C** organisation (`ngo-landscape.md` §5): a single Oslo entity with thematic teams, no chapter network, no member democracy, no volunteer pathway in the traditional sense. Unlike the humanitarian Tier C orgs (NRC, Kirkens Nødhjelp, SOS etc.), ZERO's funding model is **heavily corporate**: 86% from næringsliv partnerships, 10% public project funds, 4% event revenue. That makes it the closest peer to **Bellona** — same tier, same corporate-funded-advocacy model, overlapping mission space, different operating style (see §"ZERO vs Bellona" below).

ZERO is **not** a member of Innsamlingskontrollen, so the usual share-to-cause / innsamlingsprosent numbers aren't available. Figures in this profile come from ZERO's own `om-oss` page, Brreg, press coverage and Wikipedia/SNL.

Verified live on **2026-04-18** against Brreg, zero.no, SNL and press sources. Caveats and gaps at the bottom.

## What they do

ZERO's self-description is narrow and deliberate: "climate is the most important environmental issue" and all work ladders up to accelerating zero-emission solutions and opposing high-emission alternatives. The organisation does not do nature/biodiversity, consumption, or humanitarian work — that's Naturvernforbundet, Framtiden i våre hender and the Tier-C humanitarians respectively.

Work is organised into **five thematic teams (fagfelt)**, as published on zero.no/fagfelt:

| Team | What it covers |
|---|---|
| **Transport** | Road transport electrification, EV policy, charging infrastructure, heavy vehicles, aviation (SAF, electrification), construction machines |
| **Maritim** | Maritime decarbonisation — shipping, ports, zero-emission vessels, ferries, fisheries, offshore vessels |
| **Energi** | Renewables (solar, wind — onshore and offshore), hydrogen, grid, energy efficiency, cross-border power |
| **Finans** | Sustainable finance, climate risk, taxonomy, capital allocation, green investment frameworks |
| **Industri** | Industrial decarbonisation — CCS, process industries (Yara, Elkem, Hydro types), green industrial clusters |

The teams work through three main channels:

- **Policy influence** — written input to statsbudsjett (the annual *ZEROs innspill til statsbudsjettet* is a staple), Stortinget høringer, consultation responses, meetings with ministries and Stortinget fraction groups. ZERO is a regular høringsinstans on climate-adjacent bills.
- **Reports and analysis** — including **ZEROrapporten** (the annual flagship climate solutions report; 2026 edition launched 29 April 2026 at Mesh Youngstorget) and recurring thematic reports (e.g. *Én solmilliard og virkemiddelpakke for solkraft*, March 2026; *Cleaner construction machines in Europe*, December 2025).
- **Convening / events** — Zerokonferansen is the flagship; smaller breakfast briefings, partner events, Zeroakademiet (capacity-building programme).

### Flagship — Zerokonferansen

- **Norway's largest annual climate policy conference**, running since 2006 (2025 marked the 20-year anniversary of ZERO's founding and the conference lineage).
- Two-day format in Oslo; typically 1 000+ attendees.
- Audience is predominantly senior leaders from **næringsliv** (energy, industry, finance, transport), political leadership (ministers, statssekretærer, Stortinget), civil service and NGO/media.
- International speakers at 2025 edition included Michael Liebreich (BloombergNEF), Lauri Myllyvirta (CREA), Colin McKerracher (BNEF).
- Revenue-positive for ZERO: delegate fees + partner sponsorship cover the event and contribute to the 4% "event" income line in the funding mix (see Money in).
- Political relevance: the sitting klima- og miljøminister typically attends; in 2025 the minister was confirmed on regjeringen.no's calendar.

### Flagship publication — ZEROrapporten

ZERO's annual solutions-and-policy report. 2026 edition launched 29 April 2026. Covers the five thematic areas and lays out concrete measure-packages (virkemiddelpakker) rather than general advocacy.

## Where they work

Single Oslo office. Unlike Bellona, ZERO has **no international offices** (Bellona has Brussels, Berlin, Vilnius plus historically Murmansk). Work is Norway-centred, with EU/EEA engagement through the Brussels ecosystem and partner organisations rather than through an owned office.

No lokallag, no regions, no fylkes-structure. A single team of ~25 people in Youngstorget 1 covers all five thematic fagfelt.

## Money in — funding composition

This is the single most distinctive structural fact about ZERO, and the one that puts it in an unusual position among Norwegian miljøstiftelser.

### Funding mix (2024, ZERO self-reported on zero.no/om-zero/om-oss)

| Source | Share | Nature |
|---|---:|---|
| **Næringsliv (corporate partnerships, sponsorships, project support)** | **86%** | Samarbeidsavtaler med enkeltbedrifter og bransjeorganisasjoner |
| **Offentlige tilskudd og prosjektstøtte** | **10%** | Klima- og miljødep., Enova, forskningsråd-prosjekter, EU-midler |
| **Event / deltakeravgift** | **4%** | Zerokonferansen delegate fees, breakfast events |
| **Private donations** | Negligible | Vipps 20625; not a material line in the mix |

Source: zero.no/om-zero/om-oss (2024 figures, retrieved 2026-04-18).

### What this tells us

- **ZERO is a corporate-funded advocacy foundation.** 86% corporate share is extraordinarily high by Norwegian NGO standards; most comparison orgs are either member-funded (Naturvernforbundet, Framtiden), state-funded (Natur og Ungdom ~98% state), donor-funded (WWF Norge ~90%) or institutional-funded (the humanitarian Tier C orgs). ZERO's closest peer is Bellona — also ~86%+ næringsliv.
- **The partnership model is the engine.** Partnerskap structure: companies sign samarbeidsavtaler with ZERO; they get engagement with ZERO's thematic teams, briefings, partner events and visibility at Zerokonferansen. Published and past partners across the organisation's history include **Elkem, Siemens, Scatec, Tesla, Equinor, Yara, Avinor, Borregaard, Coca-Cola Norway, Biogass Norge, AFSN** (not an exhaustive current list — ZERO publishes current partners on the site but the list shifts annually).
- **Independence principles are explicit.** ZERO publishes *ZEROs prinsipper for økonomisk støtte* (zero.no/zeros-prinsipper-for-okonomisk-stotte) laying out: no single partner above a threshold share of revenue; no consultant contracts (ZERO doesn't take konsulentoppdrag — this is the hardest line); right to set its own policy positions regardless of partner views; public disclosure of supporters.
- **10% offentlige** is project-specific, not core ramme. ZERO does not have a Norad rammeavtale or UD frame; public money comes through specific prosjektmidler (Enova, Forskningsrådet, EU Horizon/LIFE, klima- og miljødep. specific allocations).
- **4% event** is small in absolute terms but matters strategically: it's what makes Zerokonferansen financially self-sustaining rather than a cost centre.

### Why the corporate-heavy model is structurally defensible (and controversial)

The argument ZERO (and Bellona) make: to shift **emissions at industrial scale**, you need to work with the emitters, the capital allocators and the infrastructure providers. A campaigning model funded by members giving NOK 300/year can produce protest and narrative; a corporate-partnership model can produce detailed sector decarbonisation roadmaps with credible counterparties willing to co-sign them. The 86% figure is the operationalisation of that theory of change.

The critique (periodically voiced in Norwegian media, including from other parts of the environmental movement): corporate funders, even without consultant contracts, shape the topics ZERO picks, the solutions it emphasises (CCS, hydrogen, electrification vs. degrowth, demand reduction) and the tone of critique. ZERO's counter: published principles, transparency about partners, and a track record of pushing policies (EV tax regime, CO2-avgift ramp, industrial decarbonisation schemes) that have cost partner industries money.

For the app's Funding-transparency surface, ZERO is a useful anchor for the "corporate-funded advocacy" archetype — alongside Bellona.

## Money out — what the budget buys

ZERO is small enough that the cost structure is predominantly staff (ca. 25 FTEs × avg loaded cost) plus Zerokonferansen production plus publication/report costs. No field operations, no grants-out, no large capital. A rough breakdown (not published by ZERO; inferred):

- ~70–75% personnel (25 staff × ~1.2 m NOK loaded = ~30 m, which aligns with the ~NOK 30 m income estimate)
- ~15–20% Zerokonferansen + events production (offset by 4% event income + partner sponsorship)
- ~5–10% reports, travel, office, other

Exact split would require the årsregnskap (filed to Brreg Regnskapsregisteret, not Innsamlingskontrollen).

## How to engage

ZERO is a **corporate-engagement-first** organisation. The Norwegian public's interaction with ZERO is lighter than with a membership NGO: there's no medlemskap, no fadder, no lokallag, no volunteer rota.

Verified pathways:

| Pathway | Route | Note |
|---|---|---|
| **Corporate partnership (bli samarbeidspartner)** | zero.no → "Bli partner" contact form | Primary ask. Fee scale not public; individually negotiated |
| **Newsletter** | zero.no signup | Climate-solutions updates, report launches |
| **Zerokonferansen attendance** | konferanse.zero.no | Paid delegate ticket (næringsliv rate) or partner-included |
| **Zerokonferansen volunteering** | zero.no/zerokonferansen → volunteer signup | Informal event volunteering, the one "gi tid" pathway ZERO exposes |
| **Zeroakademiet** | zero.no — capacity-building programme | Education/training format |
| **Individual donation** | Vipps 20625 | Not a material revenue line but available |
| **Tax deduction** | 500–25 000 NOK qualifies for skattefradrag | Same Norwegian rules as other godkjente orgs |

No member-democracy, no general assembly vote, no election of styreleder by members (stiftelse — board is self-perpetuating under stiftelsesloven oversight by Stiftelsestilsynet).

Previously Special Advisor (grønn konkurransekraft) in Klima- og miljødepartementet, and before that leader of carbon market and climate analysis at Thomson Reuters Point Carbon. ## ZERO vs Bellona — same tier, same funding model, different style

The two organisations occupy the same structural slot: Oslo-based climate-focused stiftelser, no chapters, corporate-partnership funding model in the 86–90% range, heavy policy engagement, no Norwegian-public activities.

| Dimension | **ZERO** | **Bellona** |
|---|---|---|
| Org.nr | 984143028 | 948778599 |
| Founded | 2002 | 1986 |
| Staff | ~25 (Oslo only) | ~37 NO, ~100 globally |
| Offices | Oslo only | Oslo, Brussels, Berlin, Vilnius (historically Murmansk) |
| Founders | Ex-Natur og Ungdom and ex-Bellona activists | Frederic Hauge + Rune Haaland (still a Hauge-led org culturally) |
| Funding mix | 86% næringsliv, 10% offentlig, 4% event | ~86%+ næringsliv, statlig prosjekt, donasjoner |
| Flagship | **Zerokonferansen** (convening, political) | Technical reports, EU policy work |
| Tonality | Coalition-building, convening, politically-oriented | Technical, investigative, willing to be combative |
| Signature issue areas | EV/transport policy, CCS, hydrogen, solar, industrial decarbonisation | Same + nuclear (Bellona is uniquely pro-nuclear among NO env orgs), Russian arctic environmental legacy, batteries, ocean CO2 |
| International posture | Norway + EU engagement via partners | Own offices in Brussels/Berlin; historical Russia work; EU lobbying in-house |

**How to read them together**: ZERO is the *event-and-coalition* arm of Norwegian climate-policy advocacy; Bellona is the *technical-and-EU* arm. They overlap heavily on sectoral recommendations (electrification, CCS, hydrogen) but differ on nuclear (Bellona yes, ZERO neutral-to-skeptical) and on willingness to be adversarial (Bellona more; ZERO more bridge-building).

For the app's Compare-NGOs surface, the two should be shown as a pair with an explicit "similar model" annotation — otherwise users searching "klima" see two similarly-sized Oslo stiftelser without understanding they're differentiated peers rather than redundant duplicates.

See also `ngo-landscape.md` §4.1 (Bellona and ZERO in the miljø tabell) and §5 (Tier C placement).

## Caveats and gaps

- **Not in Innsamlingskontrollen.** ZERO is not a member, so the usual innsamlingsprosent / formålsprosent / 5-year avg figures don't exist. Share-to-cause comparisons against the humanitarian Tier-C peers aren't like-for-like.
- **Income figure is an estimate.** The ~NOK 30 m 2024 income figure is a `ngo-landscape.md` §4.1 estimate, not a verified årsregnskap number. The actual regnskap is filed to Brreg Regnskapsregisteret and would give exact income, costs and balance — it just wasn't pulled in this verification pass. Worth doing an offline fetch to confirm and to replace the estimate with the filed figure.
- **Staff count discrepancy.** Brreg `antallAnsatte` shows 30; ZERO's own self-description and the landscape doc say ~25. Likely point-in-time vs narrative difference, or part-time/fulltime mix. Either is defensible; label the source.
- **Partner list is dynamic.** Current samarbeidspartnere on zero.no shift annually. The names in this profile (Elkem, Siemens, Scatec, Tesla, Equinor, Yara, Avinor, Borregaard, Coca-Cola Norway, Biogass Norge, AFSN) are historical + current mix. For any app surface that lists partners, fetch the live list from `zero.no/samarbeidspartnere/` at build time rather than hard-code.
- **ZEROrapporten / Zerokonferansen revenue split not public.** The 86/10/4 figures are ZERO's self-reported aggregate. A finer breakdown (per-partner contribution, delegate revenue, sponsor tiers) is not published. Acceptable for app purposes; flag if a transparency-comparison feature needs per-funder detail.
- **No country/geographic split** — not applicable; ZERO is a Norwegian-policy advocacy org with EU engagement, not a geographically-distributed operation. Unlike Bellona, no international offices to map.
- **Board composition recency.** Board list verified against zero.no/om-zero/om-oss retrieved 2026-04-18. Composition can change at annual stiftelsesmøte; confirm live before publishing.
- **Political revolving door.** Sigrun Aasland's ZERO → statssekretær → statsråd trajectory is worth flagging as context when users look at the org. It's structurally relevant: ZERO sits at a political pipeline node, which is part of what makes it effective and part of what makes critics uneasy.
