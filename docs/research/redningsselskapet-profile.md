# Redningsselskapet / RS — organisation profile

Reference profile for **Redningsselskapet (RS) — Norsk Selskab til Skibbrudnes Redning**, org.nr `954360709`. RS is Norway's civilian sea-rescue provider along the entire coast plus Mjøsa and Femunden, founded 1891 and structured as a **forening** (member-based association). In 2024 it reported NOK 913 m income and 82.5% share-to-cause. Operationally it is the largest concrete rescue-volunteer force we have mapped (1 751 volunteers in 2025) and the only Norwegian NGO whose core service is life-saving infrastructure rather than programmes.

In our framework RS is **Tier B-minus (borderline A)** — see §"Framework-fit assessment" below. Fifty rescue stations form a station-anchored network functionally similar to a chapter federation, but the stations are **not** registered as separate foreninger/FLI in Brreg. Only ten underenheter (regional HQs + Småbåtregisteret) exist under the main org.nr, which rules out the chapter-own-org.nr joins that Tier A orgs enable (Grasrotandelen, Lottstift, Regnskapsregisteret per chapter). RS gets a profile plus a Compare-NGOs slot; whether it also warrants an `activities.md` + `activity-indicator-matrix.md` depends on whether we treat stations as chapter-equivalents — argued out below.

Verified live on **2026-04-18** against Brreg, Innsamlingskontrollen, rs.no, Menon 2025 samfunnsnytte-rapport and Wikipedia. Caveats and gaps at the bottom.

## Structure — what RS actually is

RS is a **nationwide forening** with a classic three-tier governance pyramid, one national legal entity, and a station-based operational footprint:

1. **National level (one org.nr, 954360709)** — Horten HQ, generalsekretær, ledergruppe, landsstyre.
2. **Three regions** (Region Nord, Region Vest, Region Sør-Øst) — administrative, not separate legal entities.
3. **50 rescue stations** — the operational unit. 25 are **faste stasjoner** with permanently-employed crew (24/7 standby); 25 are **sjøredningskorps (RSRK)** — volunteer-crewed. Plus 1 station each on Mjøsa and Femunden inland lakes.
4. **Lokallag / aktivitetslag** — land-based volunteer chapters working on prevention, coupled to (but not identical with) station footprint.
5. **RS Ung** — youth organisation ages 13–26, with own nationwide lokallag (RS Ung Oslo, RS Ung Mjøsa, RS Ung Ålesund etc.).

### Governance

- **Representantskap** (highest decision body) — 24 district reps + 12 from user/medlems-organisations + 2 employee reps. Meets annually in June.
- **Landsstyre** (central board) — elected by Representantskap. 2025–2027 lineup: Jon Rysst (president), Nils Petter Dyvik (VP), plus Benedicte Nielsen, Morten Haakstad, Paul Chr. Rieber, Ingrid Ree Glastad, Iven Opsahl Jebsen, Trond-Olav Johansen, Kristian Nygård.
- **Each lokallag leder** has møte-, tale- og stemmerett at Representantskapsmøtet — the member-democracy channel.

## Core activities

### Primary operation: sjøredning

Round-the-clock maritime search and rescue along the Norwegian coastline plus Mjøsa and Femunden. 58 vessels; ~9 700 missions/year (2024); integrated with Hovedredningssentralen (HRS) and the 120/112 emergency system. This is a contracted statutory function under avtalen med Justis- og beredskapsdepartementet (see Money in).

### Flagship programmes

| Programme | What it is |
|---|---|
| **RS Sjøredningsskolen** (Horten) | Training school for paid rescuers and RSRK-volunteers. Courses: grunnleggende sikkerhetskurs 101, livredning, førstehjelp, brann ombord, VHF, navigasjon |
| **Sjøvettkampanjen** | Public-safety education campaign; annual awareness drive on sjøvettreglene (life-jacket use, alcohol + boat, hurtig hjelp) |
| **Sjøvettbåten / "Elias – den lille redningsskøyta"** | Children's animated educational series + travelling sea-safety events. 34 646 children taught in 2025 |
| **World Sailing RS Sikkerhetskurs** | 2-day ISAF/World Sailing-approved offshore personal survival course for fritidsseilere and regattadeltakere |
| **Forebyggende seiling** | Full school-day at sea for children — combines sjøvett, brann, førstehjelp. 2019: 6 445 children in 10 of 12 districts |
| **RS Ung** | Ages 13–26. Activity-based lokallag in Oslo, Mjøsa, Ålesund, and elsewhere. Pipeline into RSRK / maritime career |
| **RS Aktiv** | Separate activity platform / membership tier for boating and outdoor-active members |
| **Småbåtregisteret** | Registered as separate underenhet (992333677); voluntary boat register; data service |
| **Miljøvern i fjordområder** | Secondary mission under statutes: salvage, oil-spill response, miljøberedskap. Volume not separately reported |

## Money in — funding composition

This is where RS is most distinctive, and where the "40% real state dependency" claim gets sharp. Innsamlingskontrollen-reported 2024 income of NOK 913.2 m breaks down as:

| Source | NOK m | Share | Note |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| **Offentlige tilskudd** (public grants) | **365.2** | 40.0% | Dominated by Justis- og beredskapsdepartementet operating contract for sjøredningsberedskap. Falls outside `tilskudd.lottstift.no` ordninger |
| **Andre inntekter** (other income) | 283.1 | 31.0% | Includes Norsk Tipping share (~157 m in 2020, likely similar trajectory), commercial, kurs, Småbåtregisteret |
| **Medlemsinntekter** (membership fees) | 149.7 | 16.4% | ~120 000 members × ~1 200 NOK avg (støttemedlem-tier typically 350–1 600). Far higher per-member average than the ~300 kr baseline — reflects RS-Medlem's assistanse-package value |
| **Innsamlede midler** (collected donations) | 115.3 | 12.6% | Private donations, Vipps, spleis, arv, corporate |
| **Total** | **913.2** | 100% | |

### What this tells us

- **~40% real state dependency via Justis-contract.** The NOK ~366 m/yr operating-contract figure aligns with the offentlige-tilskudd line. This is a direct departemental frame-agreement for sea-rescue standby — the state buys 24/7 coastal beredskap from RS. Policy-wise it is **invisible to Lottstift**; a naive Lottstift query for 954360709 will miss the bulk of RS's public funding. Same pattern as NRC/UD or Folkehjelp/Norad.
- **Norsk Tipping speleoverskot** is large and shows up under "andre inntekter". RS, Røde Kors and Folkehjelp are the three big humanitarian recipients of the 18% "samfunnsnyttige" share of Norsk Tipping's tipsnøkkel-distribution.
- **Member fees (16.4%) are disproportionately high** vs other Norwegian NGOs. 120 000 members × ~1 200 kr = ~150 m is consistent with a medlemsmodell that gives real economic value back (free assistanse at sea). This is closer to a medlemsforening like NAF than a klassisk veldedig organisasjon.
- **Private donations at only 12.6%** is **low** for an NGO of this visibility. RS's funding base is institutional-first (state + gaming + member fees), donor-second.
- **Earlier RS self-reports of "~15% state funding"** refer to **direct statsbudsjett-tilskudd** narrowly defined, excluding the Justis-operating-contract as "oppgjør for tjeneste levert" rather than tilskudd. The Innsamlingskontrollen 40% figure is the consolidated view. Methodology matters — pick one and label.

## Framework-fit assessment — Tier A vs Tier B-minus

**Verdict: Tier B-minus (borderline A).** Reasoning, criterion by criterion:

| Tier A criterion | RS status | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| (a) Chapter network ≥ 50 units | **50 stations + ~25 RS Ung lokallag + aktivitetslag** — yes, concretely | PASS |
| (b) Chapters have own org.nr | **No** — 50 stations share the parent org.nr. Only 10 underenheter/bedrifter exist (regional HQs + Småbåtregisteret). Grasrotandelen, Lottstift, Regnskapsregisteret per-chapter joins do **not** work | FAIL (the key one) |
| (c) Volunteer + member + donate pathways all present | **Yes — all three publicly advertised with real volume** (1 751 RSRK + land + RS Ung; 120 000 medlemmer; active donor channels) | PASS |
| (d) Humanitarian/social-need indicator alignment | **Partial** — drowning prevention + sjøvett for children map to kommune-level indicators; core rescue-operation is national service | PARTIAL |

The (b) failure is decisive for the question "can we reuse the Red Cross chapter-finder code path wholesale?" Answer: **no**, because the per-chapter Brreg/Lottstift/Grasrotandelen deep-links that Red Cross, N.K.S., Nasjonalforeningen and Folkehjelp all expose don't exist for RS. A station detail page will be **thinner** than a Red Cross lokalforening page.

### Should RS get `activities.md` + `matrix.md` eventually?

**Recommended yes, but scoped narrower than Red Cross's.**

- **Activities.md**: high-value. RS has a well-defined activity taxonomy (sjøredning, sjøvett-utdanning, forebyggende seiling, RS Ung, Sjøredningsskolen, miljøberedskap) that deserves a structured document parallel to `redcross-activities.md`. Volume is smaller — maybe 6–8 activity families vs Red Cross's ~20 — but tractable. Worth ~1 week of work.
- **Activity-indicator matrix**: **partial value**. The matrix frame works for **prevention activities** (Sjøvettkampanjen → drowning statistics per fylke, Forebyggende seiling → schools with water-proximate setting, children-at-risk-indicators) but **does not work** for the rescue-operation core, which is a national 24/7 service. A proper matrix would have ~30% coverage of RS's actual activity volume. Worth building only if we also want the matrix to function as a "where is RS under-active in prevention" tool — which is Ola's kind of question, not Kari's. Build if-and-only-if we have the indicator pipeline already running for Red Cross / N.K.S. / Nasjonalforeningen.

### What's unique about RS that the framework needs to accommodate

1. **Station has a vessel.** Red Cross lokalforening has people and activities. RS station has people, activities, AND a named vessel (`RS 165 Horn Flyt`, `RS 158 Det Norske Veritas`, etc.) with callsign, position, specs. The ChapterDetail component needs a vessel-info card that does not exist in the Red Cross schema.
2. **Assistanse-as-member-benefit.** Uniquely among humanitarian NGOs, RS's membership fee buys a tangible personal benefit (free at-sea assistance when you're a boat-owner member). This distorts the "members are democratic participants" model assumed in Tier A.
3. **Near-real-time operational status.** RS has AIS-tracked vessels, live mission data via Hovedredningssentralen. A live "current RS status" surface is possible in a way it isn't for chapter-based NGOs. Tie-in with `barentswatch.md` is natural.
4. **Justis-contract dependency as a transparency story.** The ~366 m operating contract creates a single-donor-risk concentration that is politically live (see 2025 NRK Troms og Finnmark kystberedskap-debate). Worth a named callout in the Funding-transparency surface.

## Governance

- **Form**: Forening/lag/innretning (FLI), member-governed, regulated by foreningsrett and its own statutes; not underlagt stiftelsesloven.
- **Founded**: 1891-07-09. Kong Oscar II's protektion; Kristiania handelstands forening + ildsjel Oscar Tybring as initiativ-taker.
- **Generalsekretær**: Grete Herlofson (2022–2026); Janne Oddaker tiltrer 2026-03-01. Herlofson moves to Røde Kors as generalsekretær.
- **President / styreleder**: Jon Rysst.
- **Visepresident**: Nils Petter Dyvik.
- **Landsstyre** (2025–2027, 9 members + VP + president): as listed above.
- **Representantskap**: 24 distriktsrepresentanter + 12 fra brukerorganisasjoner + 2 ansatterepresentanter. Årlig i juni.
- **Regional structure**: Region Nord, Region Vest, Region Sør-Øst. Regionalization consolidated in 2020–2022.

### Memberships and certifications

- Medlem av **FORF** (Frivillige Organisasjoners Redningsfaglige Forum — see `forf.md`). Coordinates civilian rescue resources under Hovedredningssentralen.
- Integrated with Hovedredningssentralen-systemet (HRS Nord-Norge / HRS Sør-Norge).
- Samarbeidsavtale med Kystverket, Politiet, helsevesenet for ambulansebåt-drift i Helgeland/Nordland.

## Caveats and gaps

- **State-funding-share methodology.** rs.no/om-oss narrative says "~15% state funding"; Innsamlingskontrollen 2024 gives 40.0% offentlige tilskudd. Both are defensible — the 15% excludes the Justis operating-contract as "tjeneste" rather than "tilskudd", the 40% consolidates. App should display 40% with a tooltip explaining the Justis-kontrakt inclusion. Pick one and document the methodology.
- **Station list accuracy.** "50 stations" is the current consensus across rs.no/english and the 2025 Menon-report. no.wikipedia/Redningsselskapets_stasjoner warns of outdated content and lists 43–45 with a mix of categories. Needs a fresh scrape of `rs.no/stasjoner/` to canonicalise the 2026 list.
- **RSRK volunteer count drift.** 1 751 total (rs.no 2025 landing) vs 1 300 sjøredningskorps (nn.wikipedia) vs 1 141 in 2020 (Wikipedia) vs 4 200 "volunteers" (LinkedIn / older rs.no) — these are different aggregates. 1 751 is likely "all active RSRK and land-volunteers who passed training in 2025"; 1 300 is stable RSRK crew; 4 200 is total signed-up-ever. Document the definition per source.
- **Membership count drift.** "Over 120 000" (rs.no 2025) vs 111 342 (Wikipedia, 2020) vs ~105 000 (LinkedIn 2024). Membership has plausibly grown with RS-Aktiv launch and assistanse-promotion. Use 120 000 but note the definition is "paying members" including støtte + aktiv + bedrift + familie.
- **Station-level data.** Would need an HTML scrape of rs.no/stasjoner/ plus per-station subpages to pull vessel, crew-type, volunteer count, mission count. Not yet done in this verification pass. `barentswatch.md` can contribute AIS positions live.
- **Aktivitetslag count.** RS Ung has lokallag (Oslo, Mjøsa, Ålesund visible via frivillig.no and Facebook) but no canonical count. Land-volunteer "aktivitetslag" are referenced on rs.no/frivillig/frivillig-pa-land but not enumerated.
- **Pre-2025 Menon-report dates.** 2016 and 2020 samfunnsnytte-reports exist; the 2025 edition (57 pages) is the latest. Historical trend data on Justis-contract value would come from these three reports across 10 years.
- **Norsk Tipping share line-item.** 2020 figure was NOK 157 m (Wikipedia); 2024 figure is embedded in "andre inntekter" (NOK 283 m total) but not separately broken out on Innsamlingskontrollen's summary page. Full aktivitetsregnskap PDF would resolve.
